Animated SuperSource takes for ATEM switchers.

Take a SuperSource layout and the camera already on program shrinks into its box while the other boxes move in around it. Take it out and it runs backwards to whatever is on preview.

How it works

  1. Load a layout from Companion. It waits on preview, looking as it will on air.
  2. At the take, the box with the program source starts full screen, so nobody sees the cut.
  3. The boxes then move into the layout, frame by frame.
