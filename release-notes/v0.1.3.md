New starter layouts, in two groups. Still not tested on a real ATEM switcher.

## Changes

- The starter layouts now come in two groups, Default Pres and Default Grid.
- Default Pres: Slides + Presenter (the pushed version, which replaces the original), Slides + Two Presenters, Slides + Three Presenters and Two Presentations.
- Default Grid: Two Presenters and Three Presenters, each with a Push version, and Four Grid.
- In Slides + Two Presenters the slides push the side boxes, while a camera going full screen grows over the others. Four Grid and the regular presenter layouts leave the other boxes where they are.
- Two Presentations now uses two boxes at 49% each.
- Default transitions are 150 ms slower: 850 ms in and 750 ms out.
- Start with Windows now shows its tick correctly.
- The installer window and the uninstaller use the app icon.

## Updating from an earlier version

Your show is updated once when the app starts. The starter layouts are replaced with these, the old Slides + presenter is swapped for Slides + Presenter on your Active list, and layouts in the old groups move to the new ones. Layouts you made yourself are kept, but changes you made to a starter layout are replaced in this one update.
