Measures how fast the switcher plays take macros, and SS CUT and SS AUTO can replace CUT and AUTO.

## Changes

- With no layout on program or preview, SS CUT and SS AUTO now do the switcher's own cut and auto transition. Before, they did nothing.
- A macro take now waits for the switcher to finish playing it, and records how long the switcher took against the planned time. Setup, Debug, Recent takes shows both.
- Test on preview reports the two times. If the switcher plays macros slower than planned, the app rebuilds them with fewer, bigger steps so a take lasts as long as designed, and asks you to test again.
- Setup, Takes has a Macro pace setting, set by Test on preview or by hand.

## Known limits

- On the first run on an ATEM 4 M/E Constellation 4K, macro playback looked less smooth than the preview in the app. The cause isn't known yet. The new pace corrects how long a take lasts, not how smooth it looks.
- To compare, untick "Run takes as switcher macros" in Setup and run Test on preview again: it then streams the take from the PC.
