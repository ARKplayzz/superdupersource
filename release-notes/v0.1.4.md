Updates now keep your changes. Still not tested on a real ATEM switcher.

## Changes

- Updating the app never overwrites a layout you've changed. A starter layout is only refreshed by an update while it's exactly as it shipped; edit a box, name, group or timing and it stays as you left it.
- Your own layouts, your Active SuperSources list and its order, and your settings always come through an update untouched.
- Updates never delete layouts. The original Slides + presenter stays if you still have it, in Default Pres.
- Layouts you delete stay deleted.
